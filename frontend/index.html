Stikka SG – print stickers and labels from your browser

Stikka SG is the Singapore instance of Stikka, a free, open-source web app for printing stickers and labels on label printers anywhere in the world. Design a label with images, text and QR or barcodes, choose a printer on the world map, and press print. It is hosted by Edible Makerspace, an open-source DIY bio-hacker makerspace in Singapore.

How it works

  1. Design your label in the browser: image, text, QR code, Code 128, Aztec or Data Matrix.
  2. Pick a printer on the map. Each printer is an ESP32 board connected to a Zebra (ZPL), Brother QL or Seiko label printer.
  3. Press print. The finished label is sent over MQTT straight to that printer.
